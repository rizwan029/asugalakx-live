    Education

    On-chain Context

    Educational notes on how on-chain data is read by analysts.

    DEMO DATA
    Educational context only. The notes below explain on-chain concepts in general terms. They are not live blockchain data, not readings from any network, and not signals to act on.

    Exchange flows

    Net exchange inflows are often read as potential sell-side pressure and outflows as accumulation. In practice, flows also reflect internal exchange rebalancing, so single-day prints are noisy and best viewed as multi-week trends.

    Fee and usage dynamics

    Sustained fee revenue generally indicates genuine network demand rather than speculative churn. Analysts compare fee trends against price to check whether usage supports valuation narratives.

    Holder behavior cohorts

    Coins are commonly grouped by holding duration. Long-term holder supply rising during consolidations has historically been associated with digestion phases; it describes positioning, not future direction.
