Solana DeFi Activity Expands as Usage Metrics Climb

Sep 26, 2026 · ASUGALAKX Desk · Web3 Team

Activity across Solana's decentralized finance ecosystem expanded this month, with daily active addresses and DEX volumes both trending higher. Developers attribute much of the growth to the network's low transaction fees, which lower the cost of experimentation for users and builders alike.

The composition of that activity deserves attention. Memecoin trading and liquid-staking flows account for a meaningful share of volumes — segments known for sharp boom-bust cycles. Distinguishing durable usage, such as lending and payments, from cyclical speculation is the central analytical challenge.

Fee data tells a complementary story. Total fees remain modest relative to transaction counts, reflecting the network's design trade-offs. For researchers, the interesting question is whether rising usage eventually translates into sustainable economic security for validators — or whether scale keeps compressing unit economics.

Reading on-chain metrics honestly

On-chain metrics can be inflated by automated or sybil activity, and address counts are not user counts. The honest read: directionally encouraging, with the usual caveats about what the numbers can and cannot prove. One month of growth is a data point; a trend needs quarters.

Market Context. Network usage is a fundamental input, not a valuation output. Rising activity improves the fundamental picture — it does not, by itself, determine what any asset built on the network should be worth.
