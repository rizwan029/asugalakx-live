PROPOSED / DEVELOPMENT STAGE

CONCEPT PIPELINE

How a mask could become a character

Five conceptual stages. This pipeline is a design idea only — no automated 3D generation exists.

  1. 1
    NFT CARD

    The mask itself — its ID, rarity tier and visual identity from the proposed collection.

  2. 2
    TRAIT DATA

    Structured attributes: mask layer, eyes, armor, background, accessories, color and personality.

  3. 3
    3D GENERATION

    A concept step: trait data mapped to 3D model parameters — geometry, materials and aura effects.

  4. 4
    CHARACTER

    The resulting character — a distinct presence shaped by the mask's rarity and traits.

  5. 5
    ANIMATION

    Motion design: idle loops, aura behavior and environment effects tied to the aura type.

TRAIT INHERITANCE — DESIGN CONCEPT

Rarity shapes the character

Ideas for how a mask's rarity tier could influence its 3D character interpretation.

Legendary

Stronger premium aura — refined materials, commanding presence, pronounced violet energy.

Mythic

Powerful energy effects — layered aura fields and reactive particle behavior.

Exclusive

Cybernetic environment — character framed within a tech-driven scene aesthetic.

Special

Unique cosmic environment — one-of-a-kind surroundings reflecting the edition's singularity.

Honest note. Automated 3D generation is a concept and is not implemented. No character-generation system, tooling, or output exists today — everything on this page describes design intent only.
