THE SEVEN

Meet the agents

Each agent owns one stage of the pipeline — specialized, transparent, and accountable to human review.

Design principles. Human review stays in the loop at every stage. Agents surface and structure information — they never place trades, manage assets, or issue financial advice. Verification runs before amplification, always.
